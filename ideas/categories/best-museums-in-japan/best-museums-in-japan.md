# Best Museums in Japan

Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **Tokyo National Museum** - Ueno, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.tnm.jp/)
2. **Kyoto National Museum** - Kyoto - [Source](https://www.kyohaku.go.jp/eng/)
3. **Nara National Museum** - Nara - [Source](https://www.narahaku.go.jp/english/)
4. **National Museum of Nature and Science** - Ueno, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.kahaku.go.jp/english/)
5. **Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum** - Hiroshima - [Source](https://hpmmuseum.jp/?lang=eng)
6. **21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art** - Kanazawa, Ishikawa - [Source](https://www.kanazawa21.jp/en/)
7. **Mori Art Museum** - Roppongi, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.mori.art.museum/en/)
8. **Toyota Commemorative Museum of Industry and Technology** - Nagoya, Aichi - [Source](https://www.tcmit.org/english/)
9. **Edo-Tokyo Open Air Architectural Museum** - Koganei, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1635/)
10. **Hakone Open-Air Museum** - Hakone, Kanagawa - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1564/)

## Research note

- Sources accessed 2026-10-10. Verify current opening dates, access, reservations, and seasonal conditions before visiting.
