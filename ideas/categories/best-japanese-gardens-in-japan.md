# Best Japanese Gardens in Japan

Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **Kenrokuen Garden** - Kanazawa, Ishikawa - [Source](https://visitkanazawa.jp/en/attractions/detail_10106.html)
2. **Korakuen Garden** - Okayama - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/916/)
3. **Kairakuen Garden** - Mito, Ibaraki - [Source](https://visitmito.jp/en/highlight/park/kairakuen/)
4. **Adachi Museum of Art Garden** - Yasugi, Shimane - [Source](https://www.adachi-museum.or.jp/en/garden)
5. **Ritsurin Garden** - Takamatsu, Kagawa - [Source](https://www.my-kagawa.jp/en/ritsuringarden)
6. **Katsura Imperial Villa** - Kyoto - [Source](https://sankan.kunaicho.go.jp/english/guide/katsura.html)
7. **Rikugien Gardens** - Bunkyo, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.tokyo-park.or.jp/park/rikugien/)
8. **Hamarikyu Gardens** - Chuo, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.tokyo-park.or.jp/park/hamarikyu/)
9. **Suizenji Jojuen Garden** - Kumamoto - [Source](https://kumamoto-guide.jp/en/spots/detail/2)
10. **Mifuneyama Rakuen** - Takeo, Saga - [Source](https://www.mifuneyamarakuen.jp/en/)

## Research note

- Sources accessed 2026-10-10. Verify current opening dates, access, reservations, and seasonal conditions before visiting.
