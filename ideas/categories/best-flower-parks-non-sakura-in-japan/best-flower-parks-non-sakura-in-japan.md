Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **Hitachi Seaside Park** - Hitachinaka, Ibaraki - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/japans-local-treasures/hitachi-seaside-park/)
2. **Ashikaga Flower Park** - Ashikaga, Tochigi - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1473/)
3. **Shikisai no Oka** - Biei, Hokkaido - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1876/)
4. **Tambara Lavender Park** - Numata, Gunma - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1500/)
5. **Hokuryu Sunflower Village** - Hokuryu, Hokkaido - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1882/)
6. **Tonami Tulip Park** - Tonami, Toyama - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1409/)
7. **Tonami Yumenotaira Cosmos Flowers** - Tonami, Toyama - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/243/)
