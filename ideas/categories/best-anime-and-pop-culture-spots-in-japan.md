# Best Anime & Pop Culture Spots in Japan

Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **Ghibli Museum, Mitaka** - Mitaka, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.ghibli-museum.jp/en/)
2. **Ghibli Park** - Nagakute, Aichi - [Source](https://ghiblipark.com/en/)
3. **Kyoto International Manga Museum** - Kyoto - [Source](https://kyotomm.jp/en/)
4. **Fujiko F. Fujio Museum** - Kawasaki, Kanagawa - [Source](https://fujiko-museum.com/english/)
5. **Suginami Animation Museum** - Suginami, Tokyo - [Source](https://sam.or.jp/english/)
6. **Toshima City Tokiwaso Manga Museum** - Toshima, Tokyo - [Source](https://tokiwasomm.jp/en/)
7. **Toei Animation Museum** - Nerima, Tokyo - [Source](https://museum.toei-anim.co.jp/en/)
8. **Nijigen no Mori** - Awaji, Hyogo - [Source](https://www.nijigennomori.com/en/)
9. **Akihabara Electric Town** - Chiyoda, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/uk/inspiration/pop-culture-guide/)
10. **Tokyo Anime Center** - Shibuya, Tokyo - [Source](https://tokyoanimecenter.jp/en/)

## Research note

- Sources accessed 2026-10-10. Verify current opening dates, access, reservations, and seasonal conditions before visiting.
