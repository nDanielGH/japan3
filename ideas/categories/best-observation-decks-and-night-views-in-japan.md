# Best Observation Decks and Night Views in Japan

Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **TOKYO SKYTREE Tembo Deck/Galleria** - Sumida, Tokyo - [Source](https://en.tokyo-skytree.jp/enjoy/nightexperience/)
2. **Tokyo Tower Main Deck/Top Deck** - Minato, Tokyo - [Source](https://en.tokyotower.co.jp/access/faq/)
3. **HARUKAS 300 Observatory** - Abeno, Osaka - [Source](https://www.abenoharukas-300.jp/observatory/index.html)
4. **Umeda Sky Building Kuchu Teien Observatory** - Kita, Osaka - [Source](https://www.skybldg.co.jp/en/)
5. **Fukuoka Tower** - Sawara, Fukuoka - [Source](https://www.fukuokatower.co.jp/viewroom/night.php)
6. **Mount Moiwa Observatory** - Sapporo, Hokkaido - [Source](https://mt-moiwa.jp/en/)

## Research note

- Sources accessed 2026-10-10. Verify current opening dates, access, reservations, and seasonal conditions before visiting.
