# Best Industrial Heritage Ruins (Haikyo) in Japan

Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **Hashima Island (Gunkanjima)** - Nagasaki - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/japans-local-treasures/hashima-battleship-island/)
2. **Hashima Coal Mine** - Nagasaki - [Source](https://www.japansmeijiindustrialrevolution.com/en/site/nagasaki/component04.html)
3. **Takashima Coal Mine** - Nagasaki - [Source](https://www.japansmeijiindustrialrevolution.com/en/site/nagasaki/component03.html)
4. **Manda Mine** - Arao, Kumamoto - [Source](https://www.japansmeijiindustrialrevolution.com/en/site/nagasaki/component07.html)
5. **Miike Port** - Omuta, Fukuoka - [Source](https://www.japansmeijiindustrialrevolution.com/en/site/nagasaki/component08.html)
6. **Hashino Iron Mining and Smelting Site** - Kamaishi, Iwate - [Source](https://www.japansmeijiindustrialrevolution.com/en/site/iwate/component01.html)
7. **Nirayama Reverberatory Furnace** - Izunokuni, Shizuoka - [Source](https://www.japansmeijiindustrialrevolution.com/en/site/shizuoka/component01.html)
8. **Oya History Museum** - Utsunomiya, Tochigi - [Source](https://oya909.co.jp/en/)
9. **Sado Kinzan Gold Mine** - Sado, Niigata - [Source](https://www.sado-goldmine.jp/en/)
10. **Former Tomioka Silk Mill** - Tomioka, Gunma - [Source](https://www.tomioka-silk.jp/tomioka-silk-mill/en/)

## Research note

- Sources accessed 2026-10-10. Verify current opening dates, access, reservations, and seasonal conditions before visiting.
