# Best Animal Encounters in Japan

Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **Jigokudani Yaen-Koen (Snow Monkey Park)** - Yamanouchi, Nagano - [Source](https://en.jigokudani-yaenkoen.co.jp/)
2. **Awaji Island Monkey Center** - Sumoto, Hyogo - [Source](https://monkey-center.jp/english.php)
3. **Japan Monkey Centre** - Inuyama, Aichi - [Source](https://www.j-monkey.jp/lng/en/index.html)
4. **Zao Fox Village** - Shiroishi, Miyagi - [Source](https://zao-fox-village.com/)
5. **HARRY Animal Cafe** - Harajuku/Asakusa/Yokohama - [Source](https://harinezumi-cafe.com/)

## Research note

- Sources accessed 2026-10-10. Verify current opening dates, access, reservations, and seasonal conditions before visiting.
