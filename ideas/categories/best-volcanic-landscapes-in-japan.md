# Best Volcanic Landscapes in Japan

Curated shortlist of notable destinations in Japan; "best" is not a strict universal ranking.

1. **Aso Caldera / Mount Aso** - Aso, Kumamoto - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/aso-kuju/see-and-do/)
2. **Owakudani Volcanic Valley** - Hakone, Kanagawa - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1566/)
3. **Sakurajima** - Kagoshima, Kagoshima - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/603/)
4. **Mount Unzen and Unzen Jigoku** - Unzen, Nagasaki - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/unzen-amakusa/explore/)
5. **Izu Oshima Geopark: Mount Mihara and Ura Sabaku** - Oshima, Tokyo - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/fuji-hakone-izu/see-and-do/volcanic-exploration-in-izu-oshima-geopark/)
6. **Shikotsu-Toya National Park** - Hokkaido - [Source](https://www.japan.travel/national-parks/parks/shikotsu-toya/explore/)

## Research note
