## ![](https://en.nishiura.info/wp/wp-content/themes/understrap-child/img/access/anchor-illust1.png)Access to the Nishiura district

![西裏地区地図](https://en.nishiura.info/wp/wp-content/themes/understrap-child/img/access/map01.png)

1Honcho Street → Nenokami StreetThe alley across from the symbol mark "Marusaku Tanabe" on Honcho Street is called "Nenokami street." During the evening when lanterns are lit, please do enter the Nishiura District from Nenokami street.2Chuo street → Nishiura street / Ipponsugi streetComing from Gekkoji Station, if you proceed along Chuo Street, you will see the arch sign for Nishiura Street on your left. That's where the Nishiura district begins.3Honcho Street → Gekkoji StreetThe entrance on the north side of Honcho Street is Gekkoji Street. Cafes, secondhand bookstores, and more line the street. In recent years, new shops have emerged, making it an ideal destination for daytime strolls.

### Coming from Fuji-san Station

By train \| 8 minutes

Get on the Fujisan Limited Express Line at Fujiyama Station → Get off at Gekkoji Station.

3 minutes on foot

By private car / taxi \| 10 minutes

### Coming from Kawaguchiko Station

By train \| 20 minutes

Get on the Fujisan Limited Express Line at Kawaguchiko Station → Get off at Gekkoji Station.

3 minutes on foot

By private car / taxi \| 10 minutes

### Coming from Yamanakako

By private car / taxi \| 30 minutes

### Coming from Fuji-Q Highland

By train \| 5 minutes

Get on the Fuji Kyuko Line at Fuji-Q Highland Station → Get off at Gekkoji Station.

3 minutes on foot

By private car / taxi \| 10 minutes

### Coming by car

From Chuo Expressway Kawaguchiko Interchange (IC) \| 15 minutes

From Chuo Expressway Fujiyoshida Nishikatsura Interchange (IC) \| 10 minutes

### Coming by bus

By Chuo Highway Bus from Busta Shinjuku \| 90 minutes

Get off at Chuo Expressway Shimoyoshida → 15 minutes on foot to Nishiura District

By Fujikyuko Express Bus from Gotemba \| 90 minutes

Get off at Fujisan Station, then take the Fuji Kyuko Line from Fujisan Station → Get off at Gekkoji Station, a 3-minute walk.

### Coming by train

Get off at Gekkoji Station on the Fuji Kyuko Line, and it's a 3-minute walk.
