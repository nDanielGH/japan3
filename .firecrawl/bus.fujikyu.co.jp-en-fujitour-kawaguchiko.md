# Kawaguchi, Saiko, Narusawa, Shoji, and Lake Motosu sightseeing bus

## What is Kawaguchi, Saiko, Narusawa, Shoji, and Lake Motosu sightseeing bus?

The charming three-colored sightseeing buses, which depart from and arrive at Kawaguchiko Sta., are perfect for touring the local tourist attractions. From the windows, you can enjoy the magnificent view of Mount Fuji amidst the stunning natural scenery. Convenient and affordable free coupons are also available for those who want to explore many tourist spots. The vehicle design is by industrial designer Eiji Mitooka, who also designed the "Mount Fuji View Express" and the amphibious bus "YAMANAKAKO NO KABA".

## 3 lines

![Red Line](https://bus.fujikyu.co.jp/img/fujitour/kawaguchiko/fujitour_omuni_red.jpg)

Red Line

This route circles the northern shore area Kawaguchi including Kawaguchi, Oishi Park, and Kawaguchiko Herb Museum.

**Major stops:** Kawaguchiko Sta., Oishi Park, Kawaguchi Muse Museum, Kawaguchi Monkey Show Theater, and others.

[View the timetable](https://bus.fujikyu.co.jp/en/fujitour/kawaguchiko/#timetable)

![Green Line](https://bus.fujikyu.co.jp/img/fujitour/kawaguchiko/fujitour_omuni_green.jpg)

Green Line

This route connects Saiko, Aokigahara Forest, and Narusawa Hyoketsu (Ice Cave) areas.

**Major stops:** Kawaguchiko Sta., Saiko, Nenba Minshuku (Minshuku Guesthouse) Village, Saiko Iyashi-no-Sato Nenba, Narusawa Hyoketsu (Ice Cave), and others.

[View the timetable](https://bus.fujikyu.co.jp/en/fujitour/kawaguchiko/#timetable)

![Blue Line](https://bus.fujikyu.co.jp/img/fujitour/kawaguchiko/fujitour_omuni_blue.jpg)

Blue Line

This route allows you to extend your trip to Shoji, Lake Motosu, and Fuji Shibazakura Festival Venue.

**Major stops:** Kawaguchiko Sta., Shoji, Lake Motosu, Fuji Shibazakura Festival Venue, Lake Motosu entrance, and others.

[View the timetable](https://bus.fujikyu.co.jp/en/fujitour/kawaguchiko/#timetable)
