# Introduction to Autumn Colours 2025 in Tohoku Region’s Onsen Resorts – with information about the best time to view fall foliage in different regions

_Photo: Mount Kurikoma_

## **Tohoku Region**

### _Note:_

_1\. This article introduces the best time to view the foliage in each area’s onsen resorts, as well as a number of selected ryokans in each onsen area._

_2\. The best time to view fall colours listed here is based on open information published in Japan.（Survey date: November 14, 2025） Influenced by the weather and other natural factors, the actual period when autumnal leaf colouration meets its peak might be a little bit different from our prediction._
