# First Frost on Kappa Bridge as 2025 Season Winds Down

Oct. 30, 2025\[ [Getting Here](https://www.kamikochi.org/category/gettinghere), [Nature & Wildlife](https://www.kamikochi.org/category/nature), [Autumn](https://www.kamikochi.org/category/nature/autumn), [Winter](https://www.kamikochi.org/category/nature/winter), [Outdoor](https://www.kamikochi.org/category/nature/outdoor), [Advisory](https://www.kamikochi.org/category/advisory), [Weather](https://www.kamikochi.org/category/advisory/weather)\]

![](https://www.kamikochi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/frost.png)

A hearty hello to all of our loyal readers near and far!

It may only be mid-autumn for most people in central Japan, but as the weather grows chillier by the day one thing is clear: winter is coming to Kamikochi.

Just over a week ago, a sudden cold snap saw morning temperatures drop below freezing as a veneer of frost settled on surfaces around central Kamikochi.

[Five Sense blogger _Fuuta_](https://fivesense.guide/blog/today/143750/) has shared the following images:

![](https://www.kamikochi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/thermo.png)![](https://www.kamikochi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/frost2.png)![](https://www.kamikochi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/frost3.png)![](https://www.kamikochi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/bear.png)

Of course, a little frost doesn't mean that the season's over just yet. It's still possible to enjoy the site of colorful larch needles for a few more days.

As for attire, you'll definitely want to have something suitable for cold conditions, such as a down jacket, warm cloves, warm hat, sturdy leggings, and the always indispensable hiking books.

And just as a reminder to anyone who's unsure of exactly when the park closes, the last bus departs Kamikochi for Matsumoto at 17:55 on November 15th. It may still be possible to catch a taxi for about an hour after that, but don't leave it too late! You can find the full bus timetables [here](https://visit-nagano.alpico.co.jp/timetable/kamikochi-matsumoto).

[Thanks to Fuuta and all of the fine folks at Five Sense](https://fivesense.guide/blog/today/143750/) for posting the material used in our blogs this year. We'll be back with a few more updates in the lead up to closing in just over two weeks.

Till then, we hope you're all keeping warm and getting the most out of this autumn.
