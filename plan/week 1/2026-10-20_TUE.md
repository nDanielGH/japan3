# 2026-10-20 — Tuesday

## Preparation

- Have at least **10 × ¥100 coins** and **2 × ¥500 coins** available.
- Reserve a coin locker at Nagano station 2F [https://multiecube.com/en](https://multiecube.com/en)
- Get bear spray and make sure everyone knows how to use it safely.
- Check current bear sightings in **Kumamap** before leaving and again before starting any trail walking.

## Plan

### Shinjuku → Ueno

**5:41–6:08**

Take the **Yamanote Line from Shinjuku, platform 15, toward Ueno/Ikebukuro**.

- Board toward the **middle/front** of the train.
- An earlier option is to board the same train at **5:34**, arriving at Ueno at **5:59**.
- Route reference: [Shinjuku → Togakushi Shrine Okusha route search](https://japantravel.navitime.com/en/area/jp/route/result/?start=00004254&goalCode=02301-2000103&start_name=Shinjuku&goal_name=Togakushi%20Shrine%20Okusha%20(Main%20Shrine)&cid=&from=route_result_search_box&date_time=2026-10-20T18%3A34&search_time_mode=first-operation)



### Ueno → Nagano

**6:22–7:36**

Take the **Kagayaki Shinkansen toward Tsuruga** and get off at **Nagano**.

### Store the backpack at Nagano Station

After arriving, try the coin lockers in this order:

1. **East Exit, 2F** — near the Shinkansen gates and NewDays. See the [Nagano Station 2F map](../assets/2026-10-20_TUE/nagano%20station%202F.png) and [2F locker map](../assets/2026-10-20_TUE/nagano%20station%202F%20lockers.png).
2. **Zenkoji Exit, 1F** — near the taxi area outside the station. See the [Nagano Station 1F map](../assets/2026-10-20_TUE/nagano%20station%201F.png) and [1F locker map](../assets/2026-10-20_TUE/nagano%20station%201F%20lockers.png).
3. **Nagano Electric Railway, B1F** — near the underground Nagano Electric Railway gates.



### Nagano → Togakushi Okusha

**7:50–8:49**

Leave through the **Zenkoji Exit**, turn left, cross the street, and walk to **Alpico bus stop 7**. Board the bus heading to Togakushi and get off at **Togakushi Okusha**, the 12th stop.

- [Nagano → Togakushi timetable](../assets/2026-10-20_TUE/nagano%20to%20togakushi%20alpico%20timetable.png)
- [Official Alpico Nagano–Togakushi timetable](https://visit-nagano.alpico.co.jp/timetable/nagano-togakushi-green)

Bus reminders:

- You can ride without a reservation if there are empty seats.
- You can pay the fare on the bus using a contactless-enabled **VISA** card or cash.
- IC cards such as Suica are not accepted.



### Togakushi walking route

- [Togakushi area map](../assets/2026-10-20_TUE/togakushi%20area%20map.png)



#### Togakushi Okusha

**8:49–9:35**

Walk from the Togakushi Okusha bus stop to **Okusha Shrine**. Allow approximately 45 minutes.

#### Okusha → Kagami Pond

**9:35–10:35**

- **9:35–10:05:** Walk from Okusha to Kagami Pond, approximately 30 minutes.
- **10:05–10:35:** Spend 30 minutes at Kagami Pond.

Trail references:

- [Togakushi Winter Trekking Route on AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/japan/nagano/togakushi-winter-trekking-route)
- [Togakushi Land of Reverence for Mountains Course on AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/japan/nagano/togakushi-land-of-reverence-for-mountains-course)

Check trail conditions, daylight, weather, and bear reports before setting out.

#### Kagami Pond → Chusha

**10:35–11:30**

Walk to **Togakushi Chusha**, allowing approximately 55 minutes.

#### Lunch at Uzuraya

**11:30–14:00**

Eat at **Uzuraya** and take a relaxed break.

- [Uzuraya location](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JwmZu6EXf9NtNQEZ9)
- If the line is too long, try [alternative restaurant 1](https://maps.app.goo.gl/b3c8ZST9tHqzJYir5).
- If lines are still long, try [Lamp Cafe](https://maps.app.goo.gl/arMkZ9eZzbrD7tzu5).



### Togakushi → Nagano

**14:08–15:05**

Board the Alpico bus at **Chusha Daimon** at 14:08 and ride back to Nagano Station.
