# 2026-10-24 — Saturday

## Preparation

- Have breakfast before departure.
- Check the latest Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route operating information and weather.
- Bring food and water for the route; allow for limited meal choices during the crossing.
- Be ready to continue from **Dentetsu Toyama** to **Kanazawa Station** after the Alpine Route.

## Plan

### Shinano Omachi → Ogizawa

**06:15–06:55**

Take the **Alpico Kotsu Ogizawa Line bus** from Shinano Omachi to **Ogizawa**.

- Departure: **06:15**
- Arrival: **06:55**
- Arrive early enough to find the bus stop and board without rushing.

### Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route

**07:30–17:30**

Begin the **Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route** from Ogizawa at **07:30**. For now, allow **10 hours** for the complete route.

- [2026 Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route timetable](../../assets/2026-10-24_SAT/tateyama-kurobe-alpine-route-timetable-2026.pdf)
- [Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route segment map](../../assets/2026-10-24_SAT/tateyama-kurobe-alpine-route-segment-map.png)

### Dentetsu Toyama → Kanazawa Station

**17:57–18:20**

Board the **Hakutaka 569 Shinkansen** at **Dentetsu Toyama**, using **platform 13**, and ride to **Kanazawa Station**.

- Departure: **17:57**
- Arrival: **18:20**

### Kanazawa Station East Exit → KOKO HOTEL Premier Kanazawa Korinbo

**18:36–18:46**

From **Kanazawa Station East Exit**, go to **10Ban Noriba** and take the bus toward **Minabe Shako**.

- The bus departs at **18:36**.
- Count **4 stops** and get off at **Korinbo** at **18:46**.
- Continue from the Korinbo stop to [KOKO HOTEL Premier Kanazawa Korinbo](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=KOKO+HOTEL+Premier+Kanazawa+Korinbo).
