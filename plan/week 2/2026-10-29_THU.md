### Haguro-san Lower Cedar Path and Pagoda

**08:00–09:20** · Forest walk · free

> Walk the lower cedar avenue from Zuishinmon to the Five-Story Pagoda, then return to the car at 09:20.

Navigate to **Zuishinmon-mae Parking (羽黒山随神門前駐車場)**. The free public lot has about 120 marked spaces directly across from the large red torii gate and stone entrance.

An early start offers the best chance of seeing mist in the valley before tour groups arrive.

### Yudono-san Sacred Barefoot Shrine

**10:30–12:40** · Walk and barefoot shrine visit · ¥500 purification

> Walk to the inner sanctuary, complete the purification ritual, and walk the sacred path barefoot.

The shuttle is optional. From the Senninzawa resthouse, the walk to the inner entrance takes about 20–30 minutes each way. Keep shoes and socks on until the priest’s station. Allow 40–60 minutes for the walking round trip, plus the shrine visit.

- Pay the **¥500 purification fee in cash** at the priest’s window.
- Enter the open-air pavilion for the priest’s purification chant and ōnusa blessing.
- Remove shoes and socks and place them in the wooden cubbies.
- Roll trousers above the calves.
- Walk barefoot along the gravel and timber pathways.
- Follow the path up and over the enormous copper-red rock dome, warmed by flowing natural mineral spring water.
- Put shoes and socks back on, then use the roadside natural hot-spring footbath near the resthouse.
- Walk back to Senninzawa. If the road and shuttle service are operating, the optional bus costs **¥400 round-trip** and takes about five minutes each way.

### Visit Dainichibo Temple and Sokushinbutsu

**14:05–15:20** · Temple visit · ¥800 admission

> Learn about Shugendo and view Shinnyokai Shonin’s sokushinbutsu.

Pay the current adult admission of **¥800** at the office. A resident priest or attendant explains Shugendo mountain asceticism and how **Shinnyokai Shonin** practiced mokujikigyō before being entombed alive in 1783. The guided visit continues into the inner altar room, where the sokushinbutsu sits in meditation inside a glass case. Reported visits are about 60–75 minutes including the drive from Michi-no-Eki Gassan, and that range is the short end. This step keeps 75 minutes at the temple after the drive.

!!! warning **Temple rule:** Photography is not allowed inside the inner temple halls.

### Cross-Island Drive to Naruko Onsen

**15:20–16:55** · Scenic national-highway drive · about 118 km

> Follow Route 47 east through Shinjo to Ubanoyu, about 1 hour 35 minutes without traffic.

Leave Dainichibo on Route 112 and connect to **National Route 47 East (Ushu Kaido)**. The road route to Naruko Onsen is about **118 km and 1 hour 33 minutes** without traffic. Ubanoyu is just off Route 47 on the Shinjo side of Naruko Onsen Station, about a 7-minute walk or a 3-minute taxi from the station, so this step allows **1 hour 35 minutes**.

The old “105 km and 2 hours 15 minutes” figure, and the 2.5-hour allowance, are longer than this route. Two work zones are on it. At Tozawa Furukuchi, Route 47 has alternating traffic on weekdays from **08:40 to 16:30** only, through 25 December. This 15:20 departure reaches that point before 16:30 on the no-traffic estimate. At Mogami-machi Ōhori, alternating traffic runs **all day** from 5 October through 11 November. Naruko Gorge foliage traffic is a daytime problem: the trails close at 16:00 and the rest house closes at 17:00 in foliage season. This departure reaches the gorge after 17:00.

For a break about one hour into the drive, stop at **Michi-no-Eki Tozawa**, a Korean-themed roadside rest area above the Mogami River with restrooms, coffee, and snacks. Keep the stop short. The 18:00 check-in leaves little spare time once the drive is underway.
