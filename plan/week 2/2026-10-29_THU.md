## Plan

### Haguro-san Lower Cedar Path and Pagoda

**06:30–07:30** · Forest walk · free

> Walk the lower cedar avenue from Zuishinmon to the Five-Story Pagoda, then return to the car.

Navigate to **Zuishinmon-mae Parking (羽黒山随神門前駐車場)**. The free public lot has about 120 marked spaces directly across from the large red torii gate and stone entrance.

- Walk through Zuishinmon and descend the stone staircase through the ancient cedar avenue.
- Cross the red Shinkyo bridge, passing Suga-no-taki waterfall.
- Continue to the 1,000-year-old Jiji-sugi cedar and timber Five-Story Pagoda (Gojūnotō).
- Turn around at the pagoda; **do not continue up Ni-no-Saka**.
- Allow about 35–40 minutes for the return walk.

An early start offers the best chance of seeing mist in the valley before tour groups arrive.

### Haguro-san Summit and Sanjin Gosaiden

**07:30–08:30** · Toll-road drive and shrine walk · ¥400 toll

> Drive to the summit and visit Sanjin Gosaiden, its thatched roof, and Kagami-ike.

Exit the Zuishinmon lot and take the **Hagurosan Toll Road (羽黒山有料道路)**. Pay the **¥400 cash toll** at the barrier and drive 10–12 minutes through the forest.

Park free in **Haguro-sancho Parking Lot 1 or 2**, close to the summit rest houses. The two lots have more than 180 spaces.

Follow the wide, flat gravel-and-stone walkway past the bell tower to **Sanjin Gosaiden**. See its 2.1-meter-thick thatched roof, the main shrine hall, and sacred mirror pond **Kagami-ike**. Allow 30–35 minutes of relaxed walking, then return to the car and descend toward Route 47.

**[Open Haguro-san Summit in Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Haguro-sancho+Parking+Lot)**

### Drive to Yudono-san

**08:30–09:30** · Mountain drive · ¥400 toll

> Follow Routes 47 and 112 to Senninzawa, then park beneath Yudono-san’s giant red torii.

Follow **Route 47 south**, then join **Route 112 (Gassan Hanagasa Line)** toward Senninzawa and Mount Yudono. The drive takes about 45–50 minutes via sweeping viaducts and tunnels.

Turn from Route 112 onto the dedicated Yudono-san mountain access road and pay the **¥400 cash toll**. Continue to the free **Senninzawa Parking Lot**, with more than 200 spaces directly beneath the giant red Otorii gate.

**[Open Senninzawa Parking in Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yudonosan+Senninzawa+Parking)**

### Yudono-san Sacred Barefoot Shrine

**09:30–11:30** · Shuttle and barefoot shrine visit · ¥400 bus + ¥500 purification

> Take the shuttle to the inner sanctuary, complete the purification ritual, and walk the sacred path barefoot.

At the Senninzawa resthouse terminal, buy a shuttle ticket for **¥400 round-trip** or **¥300 one-way**, then take the five-minute ride to the inner entrance hut.

- Pay the **¥500 purification fee in cash** at the priest’s window.
- Enter the open-air pavilion for the priest’s purification chant and ōnusa blessing.
- Remove shoes and socks and place them in the wooden cubbies.
- Roll trousers above the calves.
- Walk barefoot along the gravel and timber pathways.
- Follow the path up and over the enormous copper-red rock dome, warmed by flowing natural mineral spring water.
- Put shoes and socks back on, then use the roadside natural hot-spring footbath near the bus stop.
- Take the shuttle back to Senninzawa.

!!! warning **Sacred-site rules:** No photography, video, or audio recording beyond the priest’s station. Observe **Katarezu, kikazu** — “Do not speak, do not ask.”

### Lunch at Michi-no-Eki Gassan

**11:30–12:45** · Roadside-station lunch · about ¥1,000–¥1,800 per person

> Drive 15 minutes down Route 112 and have a walk-in lunch at Bonji Land.

Drive about 15 minutes down Route 112 to **Michi-no-Eki Gassan (Bonji Land)** and use its free roadside-station parking with more than 100 spaces.

Order in the main cafeteria or restaurant area via the ticket vending machine or walk-in seating. Options include handmade Gassan buckwheat soba, wild mountain vegetable tempura, and Yamagata pork katsu bowls.

For an immediate alternative, eat at the **Yudonosan Resthouse cafeteria** beside Senninzawa parking before departing.

**[Open Michi-no-Eki Gassan in Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Michi-no-Eki+Gassan+Bonji+Land)**

### Dainichibo Temple and Sokushinbutsu

**12:45–14:00** · Temple visit · ¥500–¥800 admission

> Visit Dainichibo to learn about Shugendo and view Shinnyokai Shonin’s sokushinbutsu.

Drive 10 minutes from Michi-no-Eki Gassan, continuing northeast on Route 112 and turning into **Oami village (大網)**. Park free in the 50-space lot directly in front of the temple gate, then pay **¥500–¥800 admission** at the office.

A resident priest or attendant explains Shugendo mountain asceticism and how **Shinnyokai Shonin** practiced *mokujikigyō* before being entombed alive in 1783. The guided visit continues into the inner altar room, where the sokushinbutsu sits in meditation inside a glass case. Allow 45–50 minutes.

!!! warning **Temple rule:** Photography is not allowed inside the inner temple halls.

**[Open Dainichibo Temple in Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dainichibo+Temple+Yamagata)**

### Cross-Island Drive to Naruko Onsen

**14:00–16:30** · Scenic national-highway drive · about 105 km

> Follow Route 47 east through Shinjo and along the Mogami River into Miyagi Prefecture.

Leave Dainichibo on Route 112 and connect to **National Route 47 East (Ushu Kaido)**. Follow the free, non-toll highway across Yamagata, along the Mogami River past Shinjo, and over the gentle mountain pass into Miyagi Prefecture.

The drive is about 105 km and takes roughly 2 hours 15 minutes on well-paved roads with gentle curves.

For a break about one hour into the drive, stop at **Michi-no-Eki Tozawa**, a Korean-themed roadside rest area above the Mogami River with restrooms, coffee, and snacks.

**[Open Michi-no-Eki Tozawa in Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Michi-no-Eki+Tozawa)**

### Check in at Ubanoyu Ryokan

**16:30–17:00** · Ryokan arrival · parking and stay included

> Park behind Ubanoyu, check in, and settle before dinner.

Arrive at **Ubanoyu Ryokan, 65 Narukoonsen Kawaharayu, Osaki, Miyagi**. Standard check-in begins at 15:00.

Use the free guest lot behind the ryokan near the **jisui-bu self-catering wing entrance** for the easiest luggage unload. Check in between 16:30 and 17:00, comfortably before typical ryokan dinner service at 18:00–18:30.

Before dinner, explore Ubanoyu’s four distinct mineral spring sources: sulfur, alkaline simple, iron/carbonate, and sodium sulfate.
