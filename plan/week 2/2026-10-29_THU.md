## Plan

### Haguro-san Lower Cedar Path and Pagoda

**08:00–09:00** · Forest walk · free

> Walk the lower cedar avenue from Zuishinmon to the Five-Story Pagoda, then return to the car.

Navigate to **Zuishinmon-mae Parking (羽黒山随神門前駐車場)**. The free public lot has about 120 marked spaces directly across from the large red torii gate and stone entrance.

- Walk through Zuishinmon and descend the stone staircase through the ancient cedar avenue.
- Cross the red Shinkyo bridge, passing Suga-no-taki waterfall.
- Continue to the 1,000-year-old Jiji-sugi cedar and timber Five-Story Pagoda (Gojūnotō).
- Turn around at the pagoda; **do not continue up Ni-no-Saka**.
- Allow about 40–50 minutes for the round trip, including time to look at the pagoda and cedar forest.

An early start offers the best chance of seeing mist in the valley before tour groups arrive.

### Yudono-san Sacred Barefoot Shrine

**11:35–13:45** · Walk and barefoot shrine visit · ¥500 purification

> Walk to the inner sanctuary, complete the purification ritual, and walk the sacred path barefoot.

The shuttle is optional. From the Senninzawa resthouse, the walk to the inner entrance takes about 20–30 minutes each way. Keep shoes and socks on until the priest’s station. Allow 40–60 minutes for the walking round trip, plus the shrine visit.

- Pay the **¥500 purification fee in cash** at the priest’s window.
- Enter the open-air pavilion for the priest’s purification chant and ōnusa blessing.
- Remove shoes and socks and place them in the wooden cubbies.
- Roll trousers above the calves.
- Walk barefoot along the gravel and timber pathways.
- Follow the path up and over the enormous copper-red rock dome, warmed by flowing natural mineral spring water.
- Put shoes and socks back on, then use the roadside natural hot-spring footbath near the resthouse.
- Walk back to Senninzawa. If the road and shuttle service are operating, the optional bus costs **¥400 round-trip** and takes about five minutes each way.

!!! warning **29 October road and weather check:** The operator’s current notice expects the toll road to reopen in the morning but says the shrine shuttle may not begin until around 13:00 because of snow. Walking is possible only if the toll road and pedestrian route are open and safe; confirm at the resthouse before setting out.

!!! warning **Sacred-site rules:** No photography, video, or audio recording beyond the priest’s station. Observe **Katarezu, kikazu** — “Do not speak, do not ask.”

### Visit Dainichibo Temple and Sokushinbutsu

**15:10–16:30** · Temple visit · ¥800 admission

> Learn about Shugendo and view Shinnyokai Shonin’s sokushinbutsu.

Pay the current adult admission of **¥800** at the office. A resident priest or attendant explains Shugendo mountain asceticism and how **Shinnyokai Shonin** practiced mokujikigyō before being entombed alive in 1783. The guided visit continues into the inner altar room, where the sokushinbutsu sits in meditation inside a glass case. Allow about 60–75 minutes including the 10-minute drive from Michi-no-Eki Gassan; this is the short end of reported visit estimates.

!!! warning **Temple rule:** Photography is not allowed inside the inner temple halls.
